---
title: Sharing
description: Shared folders for a household, each with its own key.
---

A shared folder holds what several people use: the Wi-Fi, streaming logins,
the alarm code. It is a vault of its own, with its own key that only its
people's devices hold. The box it is kept on cannot read it.

## Your name and fingerprint

**More › Sharing** shows the name others see you by (choose it once) and your
**fingerprint**: six groups of five digits from your identity. When someone
adds you, they check that their screen shows the same digits for you: read
them out to each other, in person or on a call. That is what stops a box,
or anyone else, from slipping in a key of its own.

## Make one

**New shared folder**, give it a name, then **Add a person** from the people
at your relay, checking their fingerprint. Their devices get the folder's
key, wrapped to each of them, the next time someone in the folder opens it.

## Leave, remove, delete

- **Leave**: a member leaves; the owner's device then removes them and gives
  the folder a new key.
- **Remove a person** (the owner): the folder gets a new key and every item
  is sealed again under it; only the people still in it get the key.
- **Delete** (the owner): the folder is gone for everyone.

## Offline and under duress

Each device keeps its own sealed copy of each folder, to read offline. On
each device you can choose which folders open under the duress PIN (read
only there); the others stay hidden.
