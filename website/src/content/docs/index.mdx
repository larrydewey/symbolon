A *symbolon* was a token broken in two: each party kept one half, and the
halves fitting together proved who was who, with nothing secret said aloud.
Symbolon does the same with your keys. Every passkey you use is backed by a
threshold key: your device holds one share and a cosigner holds another, and
both must take part to sign. Sites see an ordinary passkey.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Nothing to steal in one place">
		A stolen phone cannot sign alone, and neither can a hacked cosigner.
		Your vault is encrypted on your devices; the server keeps a copy it
		cannot read.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Nothing lost with a device">
		Add a new phone from one you have, or recover with the kit you wrote
		down. Every passkey keeps working: sites never notice.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Watched, not trusted">
		Every change to your identity goes into a signed log that witnesses

	</Card>
	<Card title="Safe under pressure">
		A duress PIN opens a real but limited view and blocks lasting changes,
		with no trace a coercer can see.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
