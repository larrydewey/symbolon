## What's fixed

- **The vault locks when the screen turns off.** Before, the screen locked but the app didn't: on returning, the last opened item was still showing, with any revealed fields. Now leaving the app or the screen timing out closes the open item, hides its revealed fields and locks the vault list, whether the PIN is kept ("stay unlocked") or asked every time. The recent-apps screen shows a blank thumbnail instead of the vault on Android 13 and later.

## What's new

- **An unsaved entry survives a lock.** If the app locks while you're adding or editing an item, the editor is covered instead of closed, so you don't type it all again.
  - Unlock with your PIN or fingerprint and the editor comes back as you left it.
  - The draft is kept in memory only, never written to disk. If Android closes the app while it's in the background, the draft is gone.
  - A draft left locked for 15 minutes is discarded. "Discard" on the locked screen throws it away at once.
  - When you were editing an existing item, its page reloads after you leave the editor.

## Updating

Only the Android app changed in this release. The agent, servers and browser extension are as in 0.6.2 (the extension's version number moved to 0.6.3), so there are no new Linux binaries: use 0.6.2's. The APK updates 0.6.x in place.
