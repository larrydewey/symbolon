## What's new

- **Duress alerts.** When you unlock Symbolon with your duress PIN, your Symbolon Home box tells people you choose, with the time and your phone's last known location (a map link). When the duress window ends, or you end it from another device, they are told you are safe.
  - **More › Duress alerts**: add people, and for each choose any of an ntfy notification, email, a text message, their own Symbolon app (someone in your household on the same box), or Threnody.
  - The alert comes from the box, not your phone. Every unlock, with either PIN, sends the box the same small message, so the phone shows and sends nothing different under duress.
  - Under the duress PIN the page shows no one and changes nothing; while a duress window is open, no one can change the list.
  - **I'm safe**: on another of your devices, end the alert with your real PIN. The phone unlocked under duress cannot end its own.
  - **Household alerts**: **Receive household alerts here** lets a phone show a notification when a household member's duress alert names you. Android checks every 15 minutes and when Symbolon opens.
  - The app asks once for location permission, when you save your contacts. It reads the location the phone already has and never asks for a new fix.
  - On the box: `symbolon home alerts BOX set KEY VALUE` for the ntfy server, the mail account (SMTP over TLS), a text-message gateway and Threnody. See the guide's new "Duress alerts" page and the specification, section 14.4.
- **Ending a duress window early** now works: `device duress end DIR`, or the app's "I'm safe" button. It needs `min(policy[1], other devices)` signatures from devices other than the one under duress.
- The agent: `device beacon`, `device alerts` (`set`, `register`, `inbox`), `device duress end`, `home alerts`.

## Updating

Install the APK over 0.11.0 or earlier; it is signed with the same key. On Linux, replace the binary with this release's. Symbolon Home updates itself overnight; the alerts need its new settings (`home alerts`) before they can send anything. The browser extension is unchanged apart from its version number.

- `symbolon-v0.12.0-linux-x86_64`, `symbolon-v0.12.0-linux-aarch64`: the agent and servers for Linux.
- `symbolon-extension-v0.12.0.zip`: the browser extension.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums of the files above.

## Tests

- `symbolon-resid/tests/run.sh` on the release build: all 30 unit test files and the services test pass, including three new cosigner cases (a duress beacon opens the window once; a replayed, stale or non-device beacon is ignored; a window that runs out is reported).
- End-to-end suites that pass: cli (plain and TLS), home, share, org, rotate, breach, fill_lock, browser, ssh, fido, link, protected, bitwarden, lastpass, and the new `duress_alert_e2e`.
  - `duress_alert_e2e` runs two devices and a cosigner laid out as on Symbolon Home, with local stand-ins for ntfy and a text-message gateway (HTTPS), a mail server (SMTP over TLS) and Threnody. A normal unlock sends nothing; a duress unlock sends one alert on every channel, with the location, and to the household inbox; the settings cannot change during the window and are hidden under the duress PIN; the duress device cannot end the window; the other device ends it and everyone is told.
  - `store_e2e` failed once in the full run (its stand-in S3 server did not start in time) and passed when run again.
  - `tpm` skips on the build machine (its TPM holds a real device's keys), and `fido_usb` was skipped (no key plugged in).
- The app: `flutter analyze` finds no issues; `flutter test` passes (141 tests), including the Duress alerts page, the beacon on unlock, and the "I'm safe" button.
- Not tried yet: real ntfy, mail and text-message services, and a phone's location and notifications.
